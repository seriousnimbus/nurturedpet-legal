Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

At NurturedPet, we believe caring for your pet is hard enough without worrying about your data. This policy explains how we handle your information with the same compassion we put into every part of our app.

1. Overview

NurturedPet ("we," "us," or "our") provides a pet medication management and wellness tracking application for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our mobile application ("App"). It also covers our Care Circle community website, carecircle.nurturedpet.app (Sections 2.5 and 2.6). NurturedPet is run by The Caffeinated Poodle Company, LLC.

By using NurturedPet, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our App.

2.3 Voice and Microphone Data

If you use our Voice Companion feature:

  • Voice recordings are processed on your device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework
  • Voice recordings are never stored — they are processed in real-time and immediately discarded
  • Transcribed text may be sent to our AI service to generate responses, then cleared from memory

2.4 Phone Number & SMS/Voice Data (Care Reach)

If you enable our optional Care Reach feature for SMS medication reminders and voice calls:

  • Phone Number: Your mobile phone number is stored locally on your device and transmitted to our server only when initiating an SMS or voice call. We do not store your phone number permanently on our servers.
  • SMS Messages: Message content is generated based on your pet's medication schedule and wellness data. Message delivery logs are retained by our delivery provider (Twilio) for up to 30 days for delivery verification, then deleted.
  • Voice Calls: Care Reach voice calls deliver pre-generated audio medication reminders. Keypad responses (e.g., pressing 1 to confirm a dose) are processed in real-time and stored temporarily to sync with the app. Call recordings are not made.
  • Opt-In Required: SMS and voice communications are opt-in only. You must explicitly enable Care Reach and register your phone number within the app before any messages or calls are sent.
  • Opt-Out: You can stop all SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by disabling Care Reach in the app's Settings. Voice calls can be disabled in the app's Settings.
  • No Marketing: We never use your phone number for marketing, promotional messages, or any purpose other than the pet care communications you have opted into.

For full details on how Care Reach communications work, see our SMS & Voice Consent page.

2.5 Care Circle Website: Cookies and Page Views

This part covers only what our Care Circle community website, carecircle.nurturedpet.app, collects automatically as you browse.

  • Sign-In Cookies: The website sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.
  • Page Views: Vercel, which hosts the website, counts page views for us with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. For each view it records the time, the page's path (without invite codes or anything after "?" or "#"), the site that sent you, your browser, device type, operating system and approximate location (such as your city). It tells visits apart by a code made from your request that Vercel discards after 24 hours.
  • Why We Count: We use these counts only to see which pages people use and to improve the website. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.
  • No Ads or Cross-Site Tracking: The website has no advertising tools and no third-party tracking cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. Because of that, it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal.

Questions about the Care Circle website? Contact us using the details in Section 11.

2.6 Care Circle Website: Your Account and What You Share

  • To Make Care Circle and the App Better: to see what people use and fix what doesn't work.
  • To Support You: our team uses its own admin tools to look up your account and help when you ask.
  • To Understand Who Is Here: in those same admin tools, our team can open your account and your pets' pages, to see what kinds of pets are in Care Circle and how people take part, so we can make it better. We mostly look at this as totals across everyone. Section 5 lists what our team can see, and Section 4.3 says who can get in.
  • To Keep the Community Safe: to review reports, take down posts that break our Terms, and find abuse. It points our team to posts that may break our rules or where someone may need help, and may suggest a reply. A person decides what happens; the check never posts or removes anything.

  • Sharing Outward: if you turn on "Let others share this post" for an Everyone moment, others can share its link, and apps such as iMessage may show a preview with its first words and photo and keep their own copy.
  • Pet Pages: anyone with the link can open your pet's page, including anyone who sees an Everyone moment that tags your pet, unless you choose "Only my household" on it. The page uses the same link as your pet's Care Passport, so anyone who has one can open the other.

3. AI Care Companion & Third-Party Services

3.1 AI-Powered Features

NurturedPet includes an AI Care Companion powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you interact with the AI:

  • What We Send: Your pet's name, species, breed, health conditions, current medications, and the context of your conversation
  • Why: To provide personalized, compassionate guidance about your pet's care
  • Data Retention: Anthropic does not use your data to train their models and does not retain it beyond processing your request
  • How It Travels: AI requests are routed through our secure backend to Anthropic. Conversation history may be stored with your account so your companion can remember context across sessions
  • Spoken Replies: If you use voice features, the companion's reply text is sent to OpenAI to synthesize the audio you hear. OpenAI receives only the text being spoken, not your pet's records

Important: AI responses are informational only and not a substitute for professional veterinary care. See our Medical Disclaimer for details.

3.2 Caregiver Wellness Features & Crisis Detection

NurturedPet includes a Care Companion check-in feature that periodically asks how you are doing as a caregiver. This feature includes a safety system that monitors free-text journal entries and check-in mood patterns for signals that may indicate emotional distress.

How it works:

  • Free-text entries in the Care Companion check-in are scanned locally on your device for words or phrases that may indicate significant emotional distress
  • This scanning happens entirely on-device — your free-text entries are never sent to any server for crisis analysis
  • If concerning language is detected, the app surfaces a card with free, confidential support resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, pet loss support hotlines)
  • If you complete 3 or more consecutive check-ins reporting significant distress, a similar resource card is gently surfaced at your next check-in

What this feature is not: This is not a mental health diagnostic tool, clinical assessment, or crisis service. NurturedPet is not a mental health provider. The app cannot contact emergency services on your behalf. If you are in crisis, please contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline directly by calling or texting 988, or text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line.

Your caregiver wellness data:

  • The words you write stay on your device. Your journal reflections and anything you type into a check-in are stored on your device only. They are never sent to our servers, and the crisis scan above reads them on your device
  • A few categories do reach our servers, so we can count how the feature is used: the mood you pick (such as "steady" or "tired"), how you say your pet seemed, the scores you set in a wellness check-in (energy, appetite, mobility and pain), fixed notes you can tick such as "Vet visit today", whether you skipped a check-in, whether you opened the Care Companion, and how long a wellness activity took
  • Those categories travel on a random app code, made once when the App is installed. It is not your name and not your email address. It is not anonymous either: we keep it alongside the token we use to send notifications to your phone, and the model and version of your device. Which pet a check-in was about does not travel with them
  • Your moods and scores are linked to your NurturedPet account, so our team can see them beside your name in its own admin tools, for the three reasons in Section 5. Mostly we read them as totals across everyone rather than one person at a time
  • Your journal reflections and your Care Companion conversations are never linked this way, because we do not keep them at all
  • We never share any of this with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose other than running and improving the App
  • You can delete all caregiver check-in data from your device at any time in Settings > Delete All Data. The categories already counted stay with us, and they are not tied to your name

4. How We Store Your Data

4.1 Local-First Architecture

NurturedPet is designed with privacy at its core:

  • Primary Storage: Your data lives locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, and the App keeps working offline
  • Your NurturedPet Account: The App uses Sign in with Apple to create your account. Apple shares your name and email address with us (you may choose Apple's private relay email). On the Care Circle website, you can also sign in with Apple, with Google (which shares your name, email address and profile photo link with us), or with your email address. We use your account only to operate sync, backup, and Care Circle features, including the emails that confirm your account, sign you in and welcome you
  • You Control Your Data: The words you write in caregiver wellness check-ins and journal reflections stay on your device. The mood you pick travels as a category and is linked to your account (Section 3.2). Pet care records sync to your account as described below, and nothing is shared with other people unless you invite them

4.2 Cloud Sync & Care Circle

To keep your care records consistent across your devices and shared with the caregivers you invite, your pet care data (pets, medications, dose history, health journal entries, vaccination records, and Care Circle membership) syncs to our secure cloud database, hosted by Supabase:

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls ensure that only you and the Care Circle members you invite can read your household's records
  • Your local copy remains on your device, and the App reconciles changes when you reconnect

4.3 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Local data is protected by iOS device encryption
  • All network communications use TLS/HTTPS encryption
  • Data in our cloud databases is encrypted at rest
  • API keys and sensitive credentials are securely managed
  • We do not store payment information (handled by Apple)

Our admin tools. The admin tools described in Section 5 are a separate website of our own. They are not part of the App or the Care Circle website, and no part of them is open to the public.

  • Who can get in: only our team. Nobody outside our team has a way in, and we give no advertiser, partner or outside company access.
  • Signing in: it takes a password. Every request for information behind that sign-in is refused without it. A sign-in lasts 24 hours, and then has to be done again.
  • Wrong guesses: ten wrong passwords from one place in an hour shut that place out for a time, and fifty wrong passwords in an hour, wherever they come from, shut the sign-in for everyone until it clears. If the service that counts them cannot answer, the sign-in stays shut rather than opening.
  • What our team cannot see there: the words of your journal reflections and the words of your Care Companion conversations. We do not hold them, so no admin tool can show them.

No system is perfectly secure. We are a small team, and we keep improving how we protect your information as we grow.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide Core Services: Medication tracking, reminders, scheduling, and health monitoring
  • Power AI Features: Generate personalized insights and responses through the Care Companion
  • Send Notifications: Medication reminders, check-ins, and (optionally) encouragement messages
  • Send SMS & Voice Reminders: If you opt into Care Reach, deliver medication reminders and wellness check-ins via text message and phone call
  • Improve the App: Analyze anonymous usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
  • Provide Support: Respond to your questions and help resolve issues
  • Understand Who Uses NurturedPet: See what kinds of pets and households are here, mostly as totals across everyone, so we can decide what to build next
  • Run Care Circle: Show your posts to the audience you choose, support you, keep the community safe, and improve Care Circle and the App (see Section 2.6)

Our team's admin tools. We keep admin tools of our own, on a separate website that only our team can sign in to (Section 4.3). We use them for three things: to help you when you ask, to keep the Care Circle community safe, and to understand who uses NurturedPet and Care Circle so that we can make them better. Most of what we look at is totals across everyone, such as which species and breeds are here, how old pets are, how many pets a household has, how many medicines a pet is on, and how people sign in. When we do open one account, this is what our team can see.

In the App:

  • Your display name, how you signed in, and the email address we hold for you. If you chose Apple's private relay address, that is the address we see, and we can tell it is a relay
  • When you joined, and when you last used the App
  • Your household and who else is in it
  • Each of your pets, with its name, species, breed, age and photo, and which household it belongs to
  • Each pet's medicines: the name, the strength, the schedule, and whether it is still in use
  • Which version and build of the App you are on, and your device model
  • Your check-in moods and your wellness scores, as the paragraph below explains

On the Care Circle Website:

  • Your display name, how you signed in, and the email address we hold for you
  • When you joined, and when you were last active
  • The circles you have joined, and how many moments and replies you have written
  • Your pets' pages, with the same details as in the App. Care Circle does not hold your pets' medicines
  • Any moment or reply you have posted and not deleted, and its words, whichever audience you chose for it, "Just us" included. If you posted without your name, our team can still see that it is yours
  • Posts that people have reported, and posts the automated safety check in Section 2.6 has flagged

How you are doing as a caregiver. Your check-in moods, your wellness scores, how often you open the Care Companion, how long a wellness activity took, and whether a breathing check came out normal or worth watching all reach us on a random app code rather than your name or your email address (Sections 2.1 and 3.2). That code is made once when the App is installed, and we keep it with the token we use to send notifications to your phone, so it points at a phone even though it does not carry your name. Those moods and scores are also linked to your NurturedPet account, so our team can see them beside your name here, for the same three reasons. We read them mostly as totals across everyone rather than one person at a time.

What our team cannot see at all. The words you write in a journal reflection, and the words of your Care Companion conversations, are not kept by us, so no admin tool of ours can show them, linked to your name or otherwise. The crisis check that reads your words runs on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

What We Never Do: We never sell your data. We never use your pet's health information for advertising. We never share identifiable information with third parties for marketing purposes.
